# Dead End Drive

**The road is overrun. Your car is your last chance out.**

A top-down zombie survival driving game. Keep moving, outrun the horde, and turn your battered car into a rolling arsenal as you fight your way to evacuation.

### [▶ Play Dead End Drive](https://dkaban.github.io/dead-end-drive/)

## The game

Your mission is simple: **reach the next destination alive.**

- **Drive to survive.** Zombies pour in from every direction, and stopping is dangerous.
- **Fight back.** Your mounted turret automatically targets nearby zombies. Your car can be a weapon, too.
- **Collect salvage.** Pick up scrap from defeated enemies and choose new upgrades.
- **Build your ride.** Improve your firepower, armour, speed, and ramming damage.
- **Make it out.** Reach safehouses for repairs and push on to the evacuation point.

## Controls

**On mobile:** Touch and drag anywhere on the game area to steer and accelerate. Release to coast to a stop. Landscape orientation is recommended.

**On desktop:**

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **W** / **↑** | Accelerate |
| **S** / **↓** | Brake / reverse |
| **A** / **←** | Steer left |
| **D** / **→** | Steer right |

Weapons fire automatically. Follow the on-screen destination marker and collect green salvage to level up.
